🎓 Ilm-e-Mustalah al-Hadith (Hadith Vigyan) Sanad • Matn • Prakar • Reference Link

Koi Hadith "Sahih" Kaise Hoti Hai, Hadith ke Kitne Prakar Hain & Reference Link Kaise Add Karein?

Islam mein har suni-sunai baat ko Hadith nahi maana jata. Muhaddiseen (Hadith ke Imamon) ne duniya ka sabse barik aur scientific verification system (Ilm-ul-Jarh wat-Ta'dil & Usul-ul-Hadith) banaya taaki Nabi ﷺ ki har sachchi baat man-ghadat baaton se alag pehchani ja sake.

1. Har Hadith ke Do Buniyadi Hisse: Sanad aur Matn

Buniyad

Jab bhi aap Sahih Bukhari ya Muslim kholenge, har Hadith ke do hisse hote hain:

🔗 (A) Sanad / Isnad (Chain of Narrators)

Matlab: Rawiyon (bayan karne walon) ki woh kadi (chain) jo Kitab likhne wale Imam se lekar Sahabi aur Nabi ﷺ tak jati hai.

Misal (Bukhari #1): Imam Bukhari → Humaydi → Sufyan → Yahya → Muhammad bin Ibrahim → 'Alqamah → Hazrat 'Umar (RA) → Rasoolullah ﷺ.

📜 (B) Matn (Actual Text of Hadith)

Matlab: Sanad khatam hone ke baad Nabi ﷺ ke jo mubarak alfaaz, kaam ya taqreer aati hai use Matn kehte hain.

Misal (Matn): "Innamal-a'malu bin-niyyat..." (Tamaam aamaal ka daromadar niyyaton par hai).

Imam 'Abdullah bin al-Mubarak (RA) [Sahih Muslim Muqaddamah] ka mashhoor qawl: "Isnad (Sanad/Reference) deen ka hissa hai; agar Sanad na hoti toh jo shaks jo chahta deen ke naam par keh deta!"

2. Koi Hadith "Sahih" Kaise Hoti Hai? (5 Lazmi Shartein)

5 Golden Rules

Muhaddiseen (jaise Imam Bukhari, Imam Muslim, Imam Ibn as-Salah aur Hafiz Ibn Hajar) ka ijma' hai ki kisi Hadith ko "Sahih (Authentic)" tabhi kaha jayega jab usmein yeh paanchon (5) shartein ek saath poori hon:

1

Ittisal as-Sanad (Sanad ka Lagataar Juda Hona — Unbroken Chain)

Shuru se aakhir tak har Rawi ne apne upar wale ustad (Sheikh) se khud mulaqat karke Hadith suni ho. Beech mein koi bhi kadi (link) tooti ya gayab na ho.

2

'Adalah ar-Ruwat (Har Rawi ka Sachcha aur Muttaqi Hona — Upright Character)

Chain ka har Rawi musalman, aqil, baligh, Taqwa wala ho; kabhi jhoot na bola ho, kabira gunah aur fasiqana aadaton se paak ho. (Tamam Sahaba-e-Kiram bil-ijma' 'Adool hain).

3

Tamm ad-Dabt (Rawi ki Yaaddasht ya Likhai ka 100% Mazboot Hona — Exact Precision)

Rawi sirf nek hi na ho balki uska Hafiza (memory — Dabt as-Sadr) ya uski likhi hui kitab (Dabt al-Kitab) bilkul pakki ho—woh bhoolne ya ghalat-fahmi ka shikar na hota ho.

4

'Adam ash-Shudhudh (Shudhudh se Paak Hona — No Contradiction with Stronger Narrators)

Woh Rawi apne se zyada mazboot ya zyada tadad wale thiqah (reliable) rawiyon ke khilaf (ultha) bayan na kar raha ho.

5

'Adam al-'Illah (Chhupi Hui Kharabi/'Illah se Paak Hona — No Hidden Defect)

Hadith mein koi aisi barik aur chhupi hui ghalti ('Illah Qadihah) na ho jo zaahir mein nazar nahi aati magar bade Muhaddiseen jaanch kar pakad lete hain.

3. Hadith ke Kitne Prakar (Types) Hote Hain? (Mukammal Taqseem)

Ulema-e-Hadith ne Hadith ke prakaron ko 4 alag-alag pehluon (categories) mein baanta hai taaki har talib-e-ilm asani se samajh sake:

(A) Sehat aur Qubooliyat ke Lihaz se 4 Bade Prakar (By Authenticity Grade)

1. Sahih (صحيح — Authentic) Maqbool (Hujjat)

Jismein upar batayi gayi paanchon shartein poori hon. Iske do darje hain: Sahih li-Dhatihi (khud apni sanad se sahih) aur Sahih li-Ghayrihi (Hasan hadith jab kayi sanadon se mil kar Sahih ban jaye).

Hukm: Aqeedah aur Amal dono mein 100% Hujjat (daleel) hai.

2. Hasan (حسن — Good/Sound) Maqbool (Hujjat)

Jismein sanad judi ho aur rawi sachcha ('Adil) ho, bas kisi rawi ki yaaddasht (Dabt) Sahih ke a'la darje se thodi halki ho magar woh ghalti ka aadi na ho.

Hukm: Sahih ki tarah Hasan Hadith par bhi amal karna wajib hai.

3. Da'if / Za'eef (ضعيف — Weak) Kamzor

Jismein Sahih ya Hasan ki koi shart kam ho (maslan beech se koi rawi chhoot gaya ho ya kisi rawi ki yaaddasht kamzor ho). Za'eef ke andar Mursal, Munqati', Mu'dal, Mudallas aur Munkar shamil hain.

Hukm: Isse naya Aqeedah ya Halal-Haram sabit nahi hota.

4. Mawdu' (موضوع — Fabricated) Mardood (Jhoot)

Woh man-ghadat aur jhooti baat jise kisi jhoote (Kazzab) shaks ne ghad kar Nabi ﷺ ke naam se chala diya ho. Asal mein yeh Hadith hai hi nahi.

Hukm: Ise Hadith keh kar bayan karna ya share karna Haram hai.

(B) Rawiyon ki Tadad ke Lihaz se Prakar (Mutawatir vs Ahad)

1. Hadith-e-Mutawatir (متواتر)

Jise har daur (Sahaba, Tabi'een, Taba' Tabi'een) mein itni badi tadad ne bayan kiya ho ki aql ke nazdeek un sab ka mil kar jhoot par jama hona na-mumkin ho (jaise 5 namazon ki rak'at, Hawd-e-Kawthar, aur 'Man kadhaba 'alayya' wali Hadith). Yeh Qat'i (100% yaqeeni) ilm deti hai.

2. Khabar-e-Wahid / Ahad (آحاد)

Jo Mutawatir ki tadad tak na pahunche. Iske 3 prakar hain: (i) Mashhoor (har tabqe mein 3 ya zyada rawi), (ii) 'Aziz (kam se kam 2 rawi), aur (iii) Gharib (kisi tabqe mein sirf 1 rawi—jaise 'Innamal-a'malu bin-niyyat' shuru mein sirf Hazrat 'Umar RA se marwi hai magar 100% Sahih hai).

(C) Nisbat ke Lihaz se 4 Prakar (Qudsi, Marfu', Mawquf, Maqtu')

1. Hadith-e-Qudsi (حديث قدسي)

Jismein Nabi ﷺ farmayein ki "Allah Ta'ala ne farmaya..." (mafhoom Allah ki taraf se aur alfaaz Nabi ﷺ ke—Qur'an se alag).

2. Hadith-e-Marfu' (مرفوع)

Jiski nisbat seedhe Rasoolullah ﷺ ke Qawl (baat), Fi'l (kaam) ya Taqreer (khamosh tasdiq) ki taraf ho.

3. Hadith-e-Mawquf (موقوف — Athar)

Jo kisi Sahabi (RA) ke qawl ya amal par ruk jaye (jaise Hazrat 'Umar ya Ibn Mas'ud RA ka qawl).

4. Hadith-e-Maqtu' (مقطوع)

Jo kisi Tabi'i (Sahaba ke shagird—jaise Hasan Basri ya Sa'id bin al-Musayyib) ka qawl ya amal ho.

(D) Nabi ﷺ ke Bayan aur Amal ke Lihaz se 4 Prakar

1. Qawli (قولي)

Jo Nabi ﷺ ne zubaan mubarak se farmaya.

2. Fi'li (فعلي)

Jo Nabi ﷺ ne khud karke dikhaya (jaise wudu/namaz ka tareeqa).

3. Taqriri (تقريري)

Sahabi ne Aap ﷺ ke samne koi kaam kiya aur Aap ﷺ ne mana nahi kiya (jaiz hone ki daleel).

4. Wasfi / Shama'il

Nabi ﷺ ke akhlaq, aadab aur huliya mubarak ka bayan.

4. Sahih Hadith ki Mashhoor Kitabein (Sihah Sittah & Darjat)

Muttafaqun 'Alayh = Bukhari + Muslim

Jab koi Hadith Sahih al-Bukhari aur Sahih Muslim dono mein ek hi Sahabi se ho, toh use "Muttafaqun 'Alayh (Agreed Upon)" kehte hain—yeh sehat ka sabse uncha darja hai:

Kitab ka Naam Muhaddith (Imam) Darja & Khasiyat Sunnah.com Code
1. Sahih al-Bukhari Imam Muhammad bin Isma'il al-Bukhari (d. 256 AH) Qur'an ke baad sabse sahih kitab (~7,563 Ahadith with repeats) bukhari
2. Sahih Muslim Imam Muslim bin al-Hajjaj (d. 261 AH) Bukhari ke baad sabse sahih aur behtareen tarteeb (~7,500 Ahadith) muslim
3. Sunan Abi Dawud Imam Abu Dawud as-Sijistani (d. 275 AH) Fiqhi ahkam aur masail ki Ahadith ka sabse bada zakhira abudawud
4. Jami' at-Tirmidhi Imam Abu 'Isa at-Tirmidhi (d. 279 AH) Har Hadith ke saath uska darja (Sahih/Hasan) aur Fuqaha ke maslak likhe hain tirmidhi
5. Sunan an-Nasa'i Imam Ahmad bin Shu'ayb an-Nasa'i (d. 303 AH) Sunan mein sharton ke lihaz se sabse sakht aur pakki kitab nasai
6. Sunan Ibn Majah Imam Muhammad bin Yazid Ibn Majah (d. 273 AH) Sihah Sittah ki chhati kitab (Muwatta' Malik aur Riyad as-Salihin bhi ahem hain) ibnmajah
